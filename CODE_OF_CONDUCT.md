# Code of Conduct

We want the Scoops community to be as friendly as the Scoops themselves.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers, people from every background and people who don't speak English as a first language. German
  is just as welcome here.
- Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, and no publishing other people's private information.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or others feel unsafe or unwelcome, write to **hello@ais-con.com**. All reports
are handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions and may block people who
violate this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
